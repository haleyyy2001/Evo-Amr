# Results Summary

The detailed research summary is maintained in [research_summary.md](research_summary.md).

Headline findings from the thesis:

- Species-holdout evaluation exposes cross-species AMR generalization failures that are hidden by same-species splits.
- Evo-1-8k-base Layer 10 is the preferred extraction layer under the diagnostic suite used in this project.
- MiniRocket aggregation over ordered Evo embeddings can improve transfer for cassette-mediated resistance mechanisms.
- Global pooling remains competitive for chromosomal or diffuse mechanisms.
- The core conclusion is mechanism-dependent aggregation: representation design should match the biological structure of resistance.
